# Airspeed gauge manual checks

1. Open the Airspeed page. Confirm two circular gauges appear side by side and match the Altimeter page's visual style.
2. Check the left outer scale has ticks every 50 knots (shorter at the 50-knot midpoints) and labels 1 through 9 every 100 knots, with 9 at 12 o'clock. Check the inner mph scale has smaller ticks every 50 mph and smaller labels at each 100 mph; labels express hundreds, including 10 for 1000 mph.
3. Enter 450 knots. Confirm the editors show 517.85 mph and 833.40 km/h (editors display two decimal places). Confirm the left gauge's single pointer lies halfway between the outer 4 and 5 marks and slightly above the inner 5 mark.
4. Change each speed editor in turn. Confirm the other editors and both pointers immediately reflect the same speed without oscillation or repeated changes.
5. Check the right scale has 100 km/h ticks through 1600, and an endpoint labeled 16.7 (1670 km/h) at 12 o'clock. At 900 knots its needle should be just short of that endpoint: the exact converted speed is 1666.8 km/h.
6. Check zero, negative values, and values above 900 knots (or equivalent units). Confirm the canonical speed is bounded to 0–900 knots and both gauges remain synchronized.
7. Resize the window and switch away from and back to Airspeed. Confirm the faces, labels, and pointers remain readable and the selected speed is preserved.
